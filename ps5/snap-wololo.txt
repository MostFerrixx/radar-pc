PS5 Jailbreak – the current status
Generally speaking, the higher your PS5 firmware, the lower your chances are that a hack is available for it.
Can you Jailbreak PS5 Firmware 10.60 (25.01-10.60.00)?
Not at the moment. See below for details
PS5 Jailbreak is available up to Firmware 5.50
PS5 up to Firmware 5.50 can be Jailbroken through a Kernel exploit. Currently, people on firmware 5.50 or below can enjoy access to the PS5’s internals through either the IPV6 exploit or the umtx exploit, depending on the Firmware.
Up to Firmware 4.51 This includes homebrew support, PS4 backups, native PS5 execution, and more. Firmwares 5.00 and above are still work in progress ( details below ).

Additionally, For PS5 Firmware up to 7.60 ( 23.01-07.60.00 ) , the Mast1c0re Exploit is an exploit that leverages a bug in PS2 remakes (such as Okage: shadow King), and that runs on the PS5. In theory this works on all firmwares up to 7.60 included (the latest firmware at the time of writing), although 6.50 and 4.03 enjoy the best level of support currently.
